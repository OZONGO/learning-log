Amerigo Bonasera sat in New York Criminal Court Number 3 and waited for justice; vengeance on the men who had so cruelly hurt his daughter, who had tried to dishonor her. He watched the jury, men with mustaches and bald heads and horn-rimmed glasses, men in blue suits and men in gray, a typical American jury, selected from the ordinary walks of life, farmers, storekeepers, clerks, architects, chemists, and he watched the judge, a man with a reputation for severity, who was now listening to the testimony of the two young men.
